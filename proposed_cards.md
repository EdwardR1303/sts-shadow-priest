# Shadow Priest Card Proposals

Ten empty slots remain to reach **75 collectible cards**, including the three Legendaries and excluding the generated Enter Voidform token. Only rarity and card type are assigned below; names, costs, effects, upgrades, and rules are yours to design. Replace each `Card slot N ?` heading with a name, retaining `?` until reviewed.

Codified proposals have been removed. Accepted designs remain in [cards.md](cards.md) and [the structured catalog](design/cards.json); shared mechanics remain in [keywords.md](keywords.md). The earlier annotated proposals remain in [the review archive](docs/proposed-cards-review-2026-10-25.md).

The remaining slots are intended for curse rewards and Madness scaling, including possible overlap. No package assignment or proposed effect is locked into a slot.

X/Y/Z = **Attacks/Skills/Powers**. Heading tallies count only the ten templates in this file.

Two Power slots provide room for persistent effects while most additions remain playable Attacks and Skills. These are planning allocations, not approved card designs.
