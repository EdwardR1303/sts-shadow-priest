# Next design and playtest priorities

The class currently has 46 ordinary collectible cards, three Legendary equipment cards, and a generated activation card. If the target is 75 ordinary cards, 29 remain; treating Legendaries as extras avoids filling routine reward slots with cards that almost never appear. This is a planning target, not a requirement before playtesting.

1. **Run the current pool before adding another large batch.** Start with ordinary low-Ascension runs, then test specific decks against later bosses and the A20 Heart. Record HP lost while setting up, turn of first Voidform, cards held at activation, upgrades selected, and cards repeatedly skipped.
2. **Add Uncommons that connect existing mechanics.** Connect DoT maintenance, Madness, Mastery, defense, and burst through cards useful before the full engine is assembled. The current ordinary reward split is 20 Common / 14 Uncommon / 8 Rare; Uncommons need the most breadth.
3. **Explore energy management with a real cost.** X-cost Attacks and expensive Powers need ways to plan an energy-rich turn. Keep the choice meaningful: timing, delayed benefit, HP cost, or a setup requirement. Avoid making every engine free once assembled.
4. **Check early damage reliability.** Track whether the starter and common pool beat early elites without requiring a specific Power. A few broadly useful Attacks may help more than additional late-game multipliers.
5. **Add Rare Attack and Skill payoffs.** Build alternatives to permanent Voidform and stacked Powers. A good payoff should reward a deck choice without needing a Legendary or an exact collection of setup cards.
6. **Test the three Legendary identities separately.** Compare average results and memorable outcomes. Observe Dragonwrath's variance and repeated HP costs, Xal'atath's stat mismatch, and whether Val'anyr's retention breaks often enough to create decisions.

For each proposed card, plain English is sufficient: name, rarity/type, cost, effect, upgrade, and intended role. Continue marking unreviewed ideas with `?`; agreed designs are mirrored into the structured catalog after review.

Implementation still needed beyond cards: the designed non-starter relics, remaining placeholder portraits/character screens, and release polish. Full quit/resume playtests and complete-run balance should precede a Workshop release.
